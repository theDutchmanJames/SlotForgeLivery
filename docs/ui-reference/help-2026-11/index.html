Painting

Livery colours

Each livery has its own named colours. Paint with them, and when you change one, every place it is used changes too.

Make a colour

  1. 1Choose a colourUse the colour field, the eyedropper or a basic swatch.
  2. 2Click New colourIt becomes a livery colour. Rename it with ✎: "Gulf blue".
  3. 3Paint with itSelect a panel or a shape, then click the card.

Change it everywhere

Open a colour with ✎ and change its shade. Everything painted in it changes with it, in one step you can undo.

Two colours that should be one? Open one and use Merge into.
